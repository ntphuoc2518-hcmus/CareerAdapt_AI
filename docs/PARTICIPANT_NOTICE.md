# Participant data notice — draft for team/legal review

CareerAdapt collects account and profile information, submitted task text/files, assessment results and evidence, learning-plan progress, checklist items, reminders, and limited security audit events to provide and protect the learning service.

Submission files are currently configured for a 90-day retention period. Other records may follow account-lifecycle rules and applicable project policy. The team should confirm the retention period for each data category and the backup retention period before this notice is published.

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, payment information or unrelated sensitive personal information in task files. Submissions may be processed by an AI service after applicable privacy redaction; exact processing providers and purposes must be disclosed by the project owner before launch.

Participants should be given a contact route for questions, data export and account deletion. This draft is not legal advice and must be reviewed by the project owner before being shown to participants.
